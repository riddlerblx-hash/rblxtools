RBLX Tools

How privacy fits into the platform

Privacy Policy for RBLX Tools. RBLX Tools operates website pages, tool pages, account pages, subscriptions, and supporting content for users who want Roblox-related utility workflows in a cleaner environment. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, protected, and retained when visitors use the site.

This policy is written for transparency and website clarity. It is not legal advice. If you need legal compliance guidance tailored to your exact business or location, consult a qualified professional.

1

Information We Collect

We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed to operate, secure, and improve RBLX Tools. Depending on how you use the site, information may include:

General usage information

  • Browser type, device type, and general site behavior
  • Pages viewed, navigation patterns, and approximate usage metrics
  • Referring pages, general performance logs, and similar technical request details

Account information

If you create an account, sign in with email, or sign in with Google, we may store information such as your email address, account ID, plan status, premium status, Google-linked identity fields, and related access records needed to run the account system.

Subscription and billing-related fields

For Plus, Pro, AI token credits, and related account perks, we may store membership status, Stripe-linked customer data references, subscription status, token balances, and internal account access fields tied to your account.

Tool input information

Some tools may require Roblox asset IDs, catalog IDs, URLs, uploaded files, image inputs, prompts, output settings, or other request data to process results across tools such as Clothing, Template Background Changer, UGC, Media, Audio, Robux Calculator, Animations, and AI thumbnail generation.

We do not ask users for Roblox passwords or private Roblox account credentials.

2

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the site and its tool pages
  • Process tool requests and return relevant outputs
  • Run login, Google sign-in, account overview, and subscription features
  • Support Plus and Pro access, AI token balances, AI generation requests, gifting direction, and future member perks
  • Measure performance and understand how users move through the site
  • Protect the platform from scraping, spam, abuse, fraud, or suspicious behavior
  • Build a more trustworthy site experience than common scam-style Roblox tool pages

We do not sell personal information.

3

Accounts, Subscriptions, And Billing

RBLX Tools includes account features and may offer Free, Plus, and Pro plans as well as one-time AI token packs. To support these features, the site may store account-related fields such as plan type, premium status, token balances, subscriber status, customer references, and internal access details.

Payments, renewals, cancellations, invoices, payment methods, and related billing are handled through Stripe. We do not directly receive your full raw card number merely because you purchase through the site.

If you sign in with Google, we may use Google-provided identity details such as your email address and a Google-linked identifier to help connect the sign-in method to your site account.

Subscriber-only access may depend on the subscription state connected to your account.

4

Cookies, Advertising, And Sessions

RBLX Tools may use cookies and similar technologies for account sessions, subscriber access, analytics, functionality, abuse protection, and advertising support.

If Google AdSense or other Google advertising services are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized ads through Google Ads Settings.

We may also store session tokens locally in the browser to keep users signed in to their site account.

5

Third-Party Services

RBLX Tools may use third-party services for hosting, databases, payment processing, advertising, analytics, authentication support, anti-abuse needs, and tool functionality.

  • Stripe for payment processing and subscription management
  • Google sign-in and Google-related services where enabled
  • Google advertising services where enabled
  • Hosting, database, infrastructure, or authentication providers
  • External APIs and services needed for supported tool results

Those providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Roblox-related names, assets, UGC references, avatar items, and similar materials belong to their respective owners. RBLX Tools provides convenience utilities and creator workflows only.

7

Security And Abuse Prevention

We use reasonable technical and organizational steps to help protect the platform, accounts, sessions, and billing-connected workflows. No site can promise absolute security, but we aim to build a cleaner and more trustworthy environment than many low-trust utility pages.

  • Account sessions are token-based
  • Billing is handled through Stripe rather than unsafe direct payment collection
  • We may monitor abuse patterns, spam, scraping, fraud, or harmful request behavior
  • We may restrict or remove access when activity appears abusive or unsafe
8

Children's Privacy

RBLX Tools is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to the site, contact us so we can review and address it when appropriate.

Your Choices And Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information. You may also be able to object to or restrict some processing.

  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings
  • You can opt out of personalized Google advertising where applicable
  • You can contact us with privacy questions or deletion-related requests
  • You can stop using the site if you do not agree with the policy
