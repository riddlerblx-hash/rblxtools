Account FAQ

What can I do on the account overview page? You can check your plan, manage your subscription, update your saved profile info, and open billing tools from one place.
How do I manage my subscription? Use the subscription buttons on this page to open the billing portal, where you can handle renewals, payment methods, invoices, and cancellation.
Can I change my display name and profile picture here? Yes. You can update the display name and profile picture shown on your overview so your account area feels more personal.
What should I do if I want Plus? If you want Plus, use the plans or upgrade buttons on this page to view subscriptions and start the upgrade flow for your account.
